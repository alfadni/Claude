## Description

Masroof is a fast, private expense tracker that keeps your money data on your phone, encrypted, and offline.

- **Add an expense in three taps:** a keypad with a built-in calculator, recent categories first, and receipt photos.
- **Calendar home:** see every day's income ▲ and spending ▼ at a glance.
- **Splits and transfers:** split one payment across categories, and move money between accounts, including across currencies.
- **Budgets with alerts** at the levels you choose, and a daily "safe to spend" figure.
- **Recurring payments:** rent and subscriptions are recorded automatically or wait for your confirmation, and are never posted twice.
- **Reports:** a 12-month trend, month-over-month changes, top payees and savings goals.
- **Multi-currency:** your own exchange rates, and a previewed change of main currency.
- **Private by design:**
  - No account and no cloud.
  - An encrypted database and photos.
  - A PIN or Face ID lock.
  - Encrypted backups you control.
- **Built for everyone:** Arabic with a right-to-left layout and Eastern Arabic digits, plus screen-reader and large-text support.
